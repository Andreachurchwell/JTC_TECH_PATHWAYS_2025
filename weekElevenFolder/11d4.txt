In this class, fellows create a detailed project plan by breaking down features into manageable tasks, identifying dependencies and critical paths, estimating time requirements, building in buffers for testing, and developing strategies to manage risks and track progress

Key Terms
Milestone: A significant point marking the completion of a major phase or deliverable
Task Decomposition: Breaking down large work items into smaller, manageable tasks
Critical Path: The sequence of dependent tasks that determines project duration
Dependency: When one task must be completed before another can begin
Buffer Time: Extra time allocated for unexpected issues or delays
Work Breakdown Structure (WBS): Hierarchical decomposition of project work
Gantt Chart: Visual timeline showing project tasks and their relationships
Risk Assessment: Identifying potential problems that could impact the project
Contingency Plan: Backup strategies for handling identified risks
Sprint: A fixed time period for completing a set of tasks
Deliverable: A tangible output or result from project work
Velocity: The amount of work completed in a given time period
