This class focuses on delivering final project presentations, providing constructive peer feedback, reflecting on the learning journey, celebrating achievements, and planning next steps for career development and continuous learning.

Key Terms
Project Showcase: Formal presentation of completed work
Peer Review: Evaluation and feedback from fellow developers
Constructive Feedback: Helpful criticism aimed at improvement
Portfolio Piece: Project suitable for demonstrating skills
Reflection: Thoughtful consideration of learning and growth
Professional Network: Connections with other developers
Continuous Learning: Ongoing skill development
Career Pathway: Direction for professional growth
Technical Community: Groups of developers for support/learning
Milestone Celebration: Recognizing significant achievements
