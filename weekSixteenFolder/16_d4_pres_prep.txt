This class focuses on preparing and delivering effective technical presentations by creating compelling narratives, managing live demos and Q&A sessions, handling challenges gracefully, and engaging diverse audiences confidently.

Key Terms
Demo Script: Planned sequence of actions for demonstration
Value Proposition: Clear statement of application benefits
User Story: Narrative showing how someone uses your application
Backup Materials: Screenshots/videos for demo failures
Call to Action: What you want audience to do after presentation
Technical Debt: Known limitations to acknowledge
Elevator Pitch: 30-second project summary
Pain Points: Problems your application solves
Demo Environment: Prepared system for demonstrations
Presentation Flow: Logical sequence of presentation elements
